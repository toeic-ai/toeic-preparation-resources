# TOEIC Preparation Resources

Welcome to the TOEIC Preparation Resources repository!

This project is designed to help English learners prepare for the TOEIC (Test of English for International Communication) examination through useful study materials, practice ideas, vocabulary resources, and exam preparation strategies.

## What You Will Find Here

- TOEIC Listening practice tips
- TOEIC Reading preparation strategies
- English vocabulary for workplace communication
- Grammar learning resources
- TOEIC study plans for beginners
- Mock test preparation guidance
- Time management tips for TOEIC learners

## Who Is This Repository For?

This repository is suitable for:

- Students preparing for the TOEIC examination
- English language learners
- Job seekers who want to improve their English proficiency
- Professionals interested in workplace English communication

## Online TOEIC Practice

Alongside these educational resources, learners can explore [TOEIC.ai](https://toeic.ai/) for online TOEIC practice and mock test opportunities.

## Learning Goal

The goal of this project is to make TOEIC preparation resources easier to discover, understand, and use.

Practice consistently, review your mistakes, and keep improving your English skills.
